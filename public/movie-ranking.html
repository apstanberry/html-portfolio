Arielle's Top Favorite Series

Arielle's series recommendations for your next silver screen marathon.

1. Sherlock

In the mood for some classic mystery with a modern twist? Sherlock will always keep you wanting more.

2. Downton Abbey

You better be ready for a dynamic story, class, and some dry British humour with this period piece.

3. Reacher

Lee Child's Jack Reacher has come alive in this live-action take of adventure, action, and mystery.
